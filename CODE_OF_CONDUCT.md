# Code of Conduct

Signet Protocol brings together people who play, modify and build very different games. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background, identity or the game they love.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback; criticising ideas, not people.
- Assuming good faith and asking when something is unclear.
- Crediting other people's work.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Pushing the project to break a game's license or EULA, cheat in online games or share pirated content (see the rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Scope

This applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review) and to public spaces when someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks it.

To report a problem, send a direct message to [@kian_cx on X](https://x.com/kian_cx) or use GitHub's private "Report content" option. Reports are handled privately and with respect for the people involved.
